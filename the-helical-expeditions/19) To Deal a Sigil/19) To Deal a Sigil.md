#### **Adventure Description:** 
When the party find themselves needing a Magia Sigil to get into Magia's innermost districts, and needing one fast, they must look to alternative means of acquiring one - a shady deal in an overcrowded bazaar. 

#### **Starting the Adventure:** 
*"You need a Magia Sigil, and fast. The normal bureaucratic means of applying and waiting for consideration is far too slow and you're far too broke to afford the exclusive "expedited route." Sigils are heavily controlled, and the illicit trade of them is heavily punished. Despite this, rumors have it that one can 'acquire' a Sigil in the Shard's Guild markets through a specific contact. Desperate, you've made your way to the most prominent bazaar in Magia, the Sub-Barrier Market, so named for being literally underneath the Magia Barrier. It is a densely populated cluster of buildings with smoke and intentionally obfuscating silks abundant. Patrolled heavily by the Guild with watchtowers around, illicit deals are forced under wraps in dark alleyways here. With no name nor profile of the Dealer, your work in the Bazaar begins in earnest."*

While the read-aloud indicates the party is needing to acquire a Sigil illegally for themselves, this adventure can take on many different forms. For example, a dubious patron could have hired the party as a middleman or the Shard Guild has hired the party to capture the Dealer alive.
#### The Thing You're Here For
- Magia Sigil (3 Gems)
		- The innermost parts of the city are cut off from the rest by a powerful magical barrier. Only those with the Magia Sigil can move freely through it. 
		- An authentic sigil has 3 well-known properties:
			- **Jellyfying:** Holding the item will prompt an Insight Check or become Jellyfied. The Holder is aware of the magic when it occurs, even on a Success.
			- **Moist-smoking:** pouring water over it emits thick smoke clouds, causing the immediate Area to become Obscured.
			- **Anti-magic:** Once per Day, it can nullify 1 Source of minor magic (i.e., from Basic Abilities) within 2 Areas.

#### The Sub-Barrier Market [Adventure Map]

> Map of the Bazaar. Art by [1pagedungeons](https://1pagedungeons.itch.io/).

- **Locations:**
	- **1. The Streets:** Overcrowded with people. Visibility is limited. Each Area is Isolated when trying to run through them. 4 Patrols.
	- **2. The Watchtowers:** Each watchtower has 2 Lookouts on the roof. 2 Patrols guard the chest inside, containing Patrol Outfits and 50C each.
	- **3: Warehouse:** Ruffians roam here, jesting anyone coming through.
	- **4: Dealer and Imposters:** On the map, randomly determine the stalls where each are located.
- **Guards of the Bazaar:**
	- Hired peacekeepers within the Market, loyal to the Shard's Guild.
	- Guards share **Suspicion**. Noticeable or hostile actions increase it. The Market opening fresh fr the day decreases it by one. Start without any **Suspicion**.
		- 1: You were seen in the Bazaar.
		- 2: Lookouts track your movements.
		- 3: Pairs of Patrols follow you around.
		- 4: All Lookouts/Patrols are hostile.
	- **Lookout** [Scout Drone]
		- Camouflaged. Boasting. Acrobatic.
		- **Eagle-eyed**, sees the entire Bazaar.
		- If hostile, shine marking pointers (Ability: Target Acquired) at party members.
	- **Patrol** [Peacekeeper]
		- Decisive. Sturdy. Guild adornments.
		- **On the streets**, overseeing deals.
- **Life of the Market:**
	- Ruffian [Delinquent Goon]
		- Under-fed. Dismissive. Quick to run.
		- **Know** one descriptor of the Dealer each. **Share** it for a random cost: a custom trinket for their mother, a Patrol's feathered helmet, a specific vendor's head.
	- Dealer [Assassin]
		- Tenebrate. Blond. Moustached.
		- **Sells** the Magia Sigil.
- **The Imposters:**
	- 3 disguised Patrols pretend to be selling Magia Sigils. They stock incomplete replicas, and raise **Suspicion** levels on anyone showing interest in them.
		- Sells Jellyfying Sigils. Vendor is moustached and blond but a Chib.
		- Sells Moist-Smoking Sigils. Vendor is blond and a Tenebrate but no moustache.
		- Sells Anti-Magic Sigils. Vendor is a Tenebrate and moustached.
