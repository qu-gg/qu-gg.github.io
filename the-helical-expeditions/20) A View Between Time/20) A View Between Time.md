#### **Adventure Description:** 
The Head Field Researcher of the Helical Archive has sought the aid of the party after believing to have found the buried site of a mythical relic of the Divine Rulers within the Eaten Isle.
#### **Starting the Adventure:** 
*"Once again, as many times before, your skyship touches down and you are immediately on alert from the deadly surroundings. This expedition, you find yourselves on the Eaten Isle - the wasteland of the infamous ritual gone wrong. Sought out for your capabilities, the Head Field Researcher of the Helical Archive named Limit Testing has contracted you to help investigate the supposed site of an ancient Divine Ruler relic - a telescope that can see the past and future of whatever it is pointed at. Past the writhing flora, you see a rather plain bunker-like entrance into the mountainside ahead of you. Strapping your gear on, another heist yet awaits."*

There is plenty of scope in this Adventure Site to have fun with the weight distribution idea. The players shouldn't know anything about the tilt mechanism (unless one is clever with the Raven's riddles) until they decide to move from Node 2 to either 3 or 5. Start with some rumblings underfoot, a feeling of vertigo, etc to set the tone. After a tilt or two, the penny will drop that they somehow need to head down the corridors at roughly the same rate and weight. It is up to you if they can hear each other to communicate between the paths or not.

### Secrets & Clues:
- Set within the Profaned Oculory, ancient scrying facility of Divine Ruler servants as a means to foresee, and influence, the future.
- Purposefully built at the height of the Eaten Isle such that it could look down and observe the Divine Rulers' favorite location.
- Built at the height of mechanical engineering, the entire facility can rotate on a Y-axis running through 1-2-4-6-7 via hidden, vast gryoscopic hubs.
- When the scryers started to foretell the Heaven's Seal and fall of the Divine Rulers, and nothing the Rulers were doing affected the forecast, the Clockwork Creator executed all the scryers. 
- To ensure the creation lived on in the Rulers' absence, the Clockwork Creator ripped out its Heart, embedding it into the Device, and declared this "their last creation". Even now, it beats rhythmically.
- The Divine Ruler who built this is now lost to general knowledge. In their time, they were known as the Clockwork Creator, a Divine Ruler who dedicated themselves to grand clockwork creations in an attempt to replicate the entire Outer World in metallic miniature.
- A portion of Akenia's prowess in wielding Magitech stems from learning derived from the mechanical ruins of the Clockwork Creator in Yeso.

### Locations
1. **Ancient Security Station:** The entrance-way is mostly non-descript, built to intentionally look like a bunker. Inside, Cramped Pathways funnel the party one-by-one through ancient, still functioning security devices. 
	1. Shattered glass barriers reveal operating desks behind them, with clearance badges of ancient researchers available.
	2. Failure to present a badge results in a Smash Walls Trap (CLICK!) for the front-most party member.
2. **Cracked Plinth:** The tunnel opens up to a foray inside the machine, a small disk platform seemingly floating over an endless void. A massive **Clockwork Raven** [Grim Wing] perches on a cracked plinth, whirring as it recites riddles on "level headedness". Its wings conceal razored edges for those who seek it harm. 
3. **Mural Room:** A gorgeous mural fills the entirety of this circular chamber, depicting an unknown Divine Ruler creating this device. "The Clockwork Creator's ~~Greatest~~ Last Creation" is written in Bright Speech on a plague underneath, defaced. Slumped against the mural is Fio [Proudhound], a Chib Mana Blight Raider nursing a twisted ankle. "You! You did this to me!"
4. **Central Library:** Located centrally over the churning mechanisms, a small clerical desk and library sits here in this square chamber. 
	1. The spirit of the executed head scryer [Akaobata] persist here, waiting eternally for new knowledge. If threatened, it will summon other spirits [Yeeti] that can throw PCs and cause the Device to lurch!
	2. Information (e.g., Secrets & Clues) can be discovered here as text here is preserved in gears and motion.
	3. **Loot:** A Potion of Chrono Creamer and an Aetheric Monocle.
5. **Repair Quarters:** All manner of complicated surgical beds are within. Inert, rusted Clockwork Solders litter the floor. Davine [Proudhound], a Tenebrate Mana Blight Raider leans on a halberd, bitterly muttering about "that idiot Fio". 
6. **The Clockwork Heart:** The walls of this chamber are lined with massive tubes that creak and groan. Suspended in the center of this circular chamber is a massive, still-beating **Clockwork Heart** [Atropol]. 2 **Clockwork Spiders** [Skele-Master] crawl out of some tubes as the door slams shut behind the party!
7. **Profaned Oculory:** When the **Clockwork Heart** is defeated, the entire complex to the south begins powering down and crumbling. The party must dash across a thin bridge over the void and reach a gorgeous, enclosed observatory atop the mountain. It houses the Profaned Oculory, holding enough power for one last usage, alongside a beautiful view of the Eaten Isle below. Limit activates the Device and the party see the results within **Quest Resolution**.

### Puzzle Mechanic:
- Must evenly split their weight (i.e., Inventory Slots) between the two paths. Each PC is their Species Weight (6/8/10 for Small/Medium/Large) + their Inventory Slots. 
- As a general rule, check for Balance after every Location Action or Combat Turn. To be in Balance, the weight split must be within 3 Slots of each other - which might require the Party to exchange some items!
- A Deftness Check is required every time the device lurches into a new position, or a Falling Injury is taken. Falling Debris (CLICK!) may also occur.
### Random Events [D6]:
- Gravity Reverses! Requires a Deftness Check or become Toppled.
- A heavy Glass Skitter runs down a corridor, making the Device tilt.
- Part of the floor falls away, leaving a gaping hole to the void below.
- Characters find a mirror that shows them at various stages in their life.
- A massive chain breaks, requiring one half of the Device to have 5 more Slots of weight.
- A voice over an invisible loudspeaker urges the party to leave in Bright Speech.
### Quest Resolution:
- The party activates the telescope as it peers over the crater of Yeso, and behold the various stages of The Eaten Isle:
	- First, they see Yeso the Splendid in its full glory during the 2nd Aeon - serene lakes set against great seats of power, the Iron King himself walking across the landscape.
	- It then cuts to the Fall of Yeso, at the moment the Divine Sigil Roads were turned against the Rulers - leaving the capital in ruins.
	- Finally, they witness the final moments of the war between Akenia and Calian, the Gigaframe Paladinius and the bio-titan Raganrok exchanging lethal blows to each other's spellcore as the screen turns white.
- Alongside the loot within, Limit rewards the party with 3 Gems and offers their services as a Dungeoneer, and their departmental resources, for whatever venture they wish next.
- With the complex crumbled, the question Limit asks is "...so, how do we leave?"
