#### **Adventure Description:** 
When a torrent of Star Plums smacks the party directly in the face, they are swiftly caught up in the latest mishaps of eternally unlucky sailor, Piti Ful. Poor Piti risks dismissal by an angry admiral with no sense of humor, unless the party can help her.

#### **Starting the Adventure:** 
*Receiving word that the vessel you were due to travel on is delayed, you’ve just settled down on a bench to kill time watching the bustle of the beautiful Portian docks. Suddenly, a number of firm, star-shaped lumps rain down on you. The projectiles burst on impact, covering your face, hair and clothes with a sickly sweet liquid. As you wipe star plum from your eyes, you see a Chib rushing towards you, waving her hands while babbling an apology.*

Piti Ful is widely regarded as the unluckiest sailor in the fleet. While her crewmates find Piti’s mishaps endearing, her clumsiness has drawn the ire of grumpy Admiral Nosewright, who is capable of putting her whole naval career in jeopardy. He has given her an impossibly long list of chores to complete by noon, less than two hours from now. If Piti fails, she’ll lose her place on the Serendipity’s next voyage, face disciplinary action and possibly be kicked out of the Confederacy for good.

#### **Suggested Story:**
1. Set the scene, describe Piti, have her apologize for spilling plums on the party, then continue:
2. **Under Watch:**
	1. Admiral Nosewright walks past, shouts at Piti and says, “This is your last chance. I don’t care how you get your chores done, just get them done!”.
	2. Once the admiral has left, Piti explains he will fire her if she doesn’t complete her chores in time. She offers to pay the party to help her finish.
3. **Shipshape and Ready:**
	1. First, the party must load supplies onto the ship and take them to Greem’s galley. Piti falls off the gangplank and must be lifted out of the deep water.
	2. Next, the party must change the sheets in the captain’s quarters. Piti disturbs a nest of 3 Bedmunk Swarms who all scream anxiously, one swarm engulfing her immediately.
4. **Swab the Decks:**
	1. The final task is swabbing the decks. Piti knocks over a heavy manafuel barrel. The barrel contains 6 Goops, which attack the party.
	2. After 1 Round of Combat, Greem rushes to the deck to help defeat the Goops. At the end of the fight, Piti falls into the empty barrel and gets stuck.
5. **Bon Voyage:**
	1. Admiral Nosewright appears, furious about the mess. After the party take Piti out of the barrel, she shows off a spyglass she has just discovered inside.
	2. Greem says Piti should be rewarded for finishing her chores, exposing onboard hazards and finding the lost spyglass. The admiral reluctantly agrees.

#### **Important Characters:**
**Piti Ful**
Chib, Female, 21 (Basic Folk)
Accident-prone Piti grew up in a family of pirate, but has always dreamed of serving in the Portian Confederacy.
***“The sea breeze has made her hair knotted and unruly, while her white sailor’s uniform is dusty and wearing away at the knees.”***

**Admiral Nosewright**
*Elf, Male, 53 (Blaster Knight)*
Admiral Nosewright oversees the missions and crews of a fleet of Confederacy ships from his offices on shore.
***"The admiral is neatly turned out, with boots, buttons and glorious nose all shining in the sun. He stands as stiffly as his heavily starched collar."***

**Sir Gerard Laughs-a-Lot**
*Gruun, Female, 36 (Custrel)*
First mate and cook onboard the Serendipity, Greem is fond of Piti, but cannot overrule the admiral.
**“This sturdy sailor is quadruple Piti’s height. She has a warm smile and tiny chef's hat.”**

#### **Key Locations:**
**Portian Docks**
***"A refreshing morning breeze lifts at tarpaulins, flying them like banners. Dock workers joke with each other, and hawkers sell wares on these white sandstone streets."***
The jetty the party were due to embark from is also where the Serendipity is docked. Twenty large crates of supplies are stacked on the jetty, waiting to be loaded onto the ship.

**Deck of the *Serendipity***
***"The Serendipity is clearly newer than any of the other docked vessels. The sails are in perfect condition and everything onboard smells of fresh varnish."***
A rickety plank leads from the dock to the ship’s wooden deck. The decks look clean, but still failed the admiral’s morning inspection. Barrels of manafuel are kept on deck to fuel the ship's Spell Engine. This is where the Goops are hiding.

**Below Decks**
***"Stepping down the smooth wooden steps into the bowels of the ship, you hear the cheerful sounds of someone singing an old sea shanty"***
The party must load all the crates into a supply room adjoining the galley, where Greem is busy cooking. The crew sleep in hammocks, but the captain has his own room with a large bed. There are rodents nesting in the linen closet.
#### Secrets and Clues
1. The admiral resents Piti because she once spilled juice on his uniform right before the [Scion's Fair](https://breakrpg.blogspot.com/2015/09/three-factoids-1st-scroll.html).
2. Admiral Nosewright is especially irate today because he has lost his lucky, handheld, Dew Silver Spyglass.
3. Piti has served on the Serendipity, a small scout vessel, for the past year after flunking the Portian Naval Academy.
4. Despite her bad luck, Piti is popular with her crewmates, due to her infectious optimism.
5. The Serendipity is about to go on its first lengthy voyage to the Sol Alliance.
6. The rest of the crew are off enjoying their last day while Greem oversees final preparations.
7. Greem is friends with Piti's older brother, Bash; she promised to watch over Piti.
8. If Piti receives dismissal from the Confederacy, she'd struggle to find work on any other ship, even with the pirate clans.
9. The captain of Serendipity takes pride in his finest Night Silk bedrobes for sleep, and these Bedmunks found it to be suitable material for their next grand work.
10. No one realized the shipment of manafuel barrels was accidentally waste-barrels instead.

#### Quest Resolution
**Rewards:** Piti offers the party 100C (her salary for the next voyage) to help her. If she keeps her job, Greem will provide each party member Star Plumcakes [Booster Cakes] as a thank you. If the
admiral is suitably persuaded via Negotiation, he will reluctantly promote Piti.

**Captain Rewards:** If the party inform the Captain of his Bedmunk infestation, he'll nearly cry (a huge, broad Promethean) before saying he'll have to get a new set. He offers the party the Unit of "tarnished" [Night Silk](https://breakrpg.blogspot.com/2025/11/wistful-dark-items-freebie-gear.html). It is magical material and still immaculate.
