#### **Adventure Description:** 
The Helical Archive has been waiting on time-sensitive orders from Cog's finest gadgeteer for a while and, having been left unusually disappointed,  they have sent the party in to scope out the situation and find out what's going on.

#### **Starting the Adventure:** 
*"Center of the junkyard island that is Stahlfeld lies Cog, the clockwork city of inventors. No inventor is known better here than Gadgeteer Inner Working, a Rai-Neko know to all on the island. It is said he can take any Gleysian scrap found in the junkyards nearby and make it a functioning marvel again...for a hefty price that is. His workshop, Working's Works, is a trusted collaborator for the Helical Archive. When orders stopped returning on-time, and at a much worse quality, the Archive dispatched you to check in. Standing on the street outside Working's Works rather humble storefront, nothing is seemingly out of the ordinary."*

This Adventure Site is fairly constrained in scope to just the workshop but there are many avenues of resolution available depending on the party's interests - from a simple rescue to exposing/blackmailing Working themselves. Stealth and Negotiation work just as well as storming the storefront, so run with whichever way the party goes!

#### Secrets and Clues:
1. The secret of Working's craft is the direct use of Gleysian Bio-Mechanoid "soul cores" in his repairs, capable of imbuing a base level of consciousness to make these ancient devices work again. 
2. Working doesn't know the specifics of how the imbuement truly works; he knows it works and no questions need be asked.
3. If the secret is revealed and Working is confronted about it, he'll become defensive and upset, claiming he only uses irreparable cores and that he has an agreement with the Archive. This is a half-truth as it's via unofficial channels with a specific researcher.
4. Working is planning to only share this knowledge of the craft with his first-born son, Drop, and is careful not to share secrets with his second, Pinch.
5. Brother Pinch, as second-born, won't inherit the forge or its knowledge. Envy towards Drop drove him to hire Kidnappers to take his "stolen" inheritance by force. He speaks to them via Hush Band.
6. The Kidnappers are a gang of unsuccessful Chib Junkers seeking to take the knowledge of Working's craft and setup a competitor in Sprocket. With Working holding out so long, their patience is running thin and they are considering robbing the workshop empty instead.
7. Both Drop and Pinch were away at the time of Working's disappearance, at Pinch's behest.

1.  **Storefront** [Entrance] [Guardian Encounter] :
	1. No customers inside. Half-empty racks of minor arcane devices (i.e., a Techatiel Build-Kit and Hush Bands). Bit of bloodsplatter on the floor. 2 Kidnappers pretend to keep shop.
	2. Techatiel Build-Kit
		*Often eerily seen staring at you off on some distant ledge or tree, these dark button-eyed birds are actually man-made contraptions, acting as mobile scouts for anyone looking to protect their territory.*
		- A Gadgeteer can leverage these kits to Craft a Techatiel of their own commanding.
		- 1 Slot | 50 Coins
	3. Hush Bands
		*After an academic report discovered that folks' vocal chords ever so slightly vibrate when they simply think up a sentence, crafty Gadgeteers made fashionable chokers allowing for, essentially, short-range telepathy.*
		- Anyone wearing a matching choker may telepathically speak to each other, not requiring vocal components at all.
		- This has an effective range of 100ft, or 3 Areas.
		- Technically speaking, it works up to 200ft, or 6 Areas. This requires an Aura Check or the intended message becomes altered against the user's intent.
		- 0.5 Slots | 75 Coins
	4. Kidnappers [Peacekeeper] : 
		- Chibs. Junkers. Impatient.
		- **Pretend** they help Working run the shop and crafts in his absence.
		- Communicate via Hush Bands.
2. **Sleeping Quarters** [Guardian Encounter] :
	1. Messy beds, as if recently searched. 2 Kidnappers whisper about the state of plans with Pinch. The closet holds a secret door.
	2. 2 Extreme Heat Outfits (Source, pg. ) within the room.
	3. Penny Drop & Penny Pinch [Custrel] :
		1. Young Rai-Neko adults. Twins. Working's sons.
		2. **Indistinguishable** from each other.
		3. **Claim** Working is missing for different reasons.
3. **Foundry**: 
	1. Hot. Constant buzzing and puffing. Low Pipes hinder movement (all Areas here are Cramped and Isolated) for anyone but Small Species. 
	2. The Area is protected by 4 Scout Drones hovering in the corners. 
	3. Unlike the more rigid repurposed Drones in Cog, the Autodrones of the workshop seem more intelligent.
	4. Autodrones [Drone] :
		1. Gleysian, remade. Surprisingly intelligent.
		2. **Uncaring** about who runs the smithy.
		3. Autonomously **focused** on their repairs.
		4. **Hostile** to anyone messing with the machinery or projects.
	5. Scout Drones [Scout Drone] :
		1. Buzzing. Refined. Laser Pointers.
		2. **Perceive** intentions of anyone around.
		3. **Attack** anyone wishing others harm.
4. **Compactor** [Harmful Terrain] :
	1. Loud Grinding of the Compactor - 2 Areas wide or 15ft across. Powered by the Waterwheel. Crushes everything.
	2. Failing an attempt to go across results in escalating Injuries, starting with a Light Injury and increasing 1 Stage per Turn. Requires appropriate use of an Item/Ability or a successful Check (at a Snag unless an external party aids) to escape.
5. **Hearth** [Harmful Terrain] : 
	1. Zone full of flames, heating Pipes and the Cores within, which must be kept moving before imbuement. 
	2. There's a door behind the flames to Working's Secret Office.
	3. Exposure to the flames directly results in a Serious Burn (Source, pg. 267) per Turn.
6. **Waterwheel** [Entrance] [Guardian Encounter] :
	1. Gadgeteer Working is bound to the waterwheel itself, a stream of water constantly turning him. 3 bored Kidnappers hold guard, playing games.
	2. Via the roof, there is technically an entrance through the waterway that powers the Waterwheel - though its a bit slippery!
	3. A door hides behind the water-powered gears, leading to Working's Secret Office.
	4. Gadgeteer Inner Working [Custrel]
		- Rai-Neko. Drowsy. Soft-spoken. Full prosthetic torso and arms. 
		- **Knows** how to build/fix Autodrones. 
		- **Knows** how to repair very complex Gleysian relics. 
		- **Unwilling** to share this knowledge. 
7. **Secret Office** [Loot] : 
	1. 2 Drones protect schematics on a desk. These papers hold the secrets to the use of Bio-Mechanoid Cores in Working's work.
	2. A hatch can open a Pipe ending here. Just inside the Pipe is an inert Soul Coul, acting as a Small Spell Engine.
#### Random Encounters [D20] :
Random Events:
	1-4: The Kidnappers in the room become Hostile, attempting an Ambush.
	5-9: 2 Autodrones short-circuit, making them attack the party.
	10-13: Drop & Pinch argue about the disappearance of Working, forcing the party to take a side.
	14-16: A faint trail of bloodsplatters points in the direction of the Waterwheel.
	17-18: Pinch assures everyone his dad has gone on a spontaneous vacation and not to worry.
	19-20: Drop is asking around if anyone has seen his dad recently.

#### Quest Resolution:
**Rescue:** On a successful rescue, Working will let the party keep anything they looted during the rescue and provide free Advanced Gadgeteering service to each PC. If Pinch's plans were exposed to Working, he will disown him but not press charges. The Kidnappers' fate is left up to the party.

**Confronting the Gadgeteer:** If the party learns the truth about Working's use of Cores, and cares about it, Working will try to cut a "hush hush" deal with the party to not inform the Archive, offering his **Sludge Wrench** up.

**Sludge Wrench** [Standard Weapon]
*Despite a hefty weight, whenever this wrench is dropped it simply floats a few inches off the floor. Looks constantly slicked with oil.*
- Magical weapon made of Sky Steel.
- Imbued with **Eldritch Ennui Sludge** (Source, pg. 292): Imbued items can slow the operation of vehicles and other moving mechanical contraptions to a virtual stop. Any machine in physical contact with the imbued item will refuse to move.
- 1 Slot | 200 Coins
