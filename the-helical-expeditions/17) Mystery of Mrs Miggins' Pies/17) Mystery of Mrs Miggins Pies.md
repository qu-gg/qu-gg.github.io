This is an adaptation of the wonderful one-shot **The Goblins and the Pie Shop** by [Grant Sheridan](https://burnafterrunningrpg.com/2018/01/08/the-goblins-and-the-pie-shop-a-1st-level-dd-adventure/).

#### **Adventure Description:**
Mrs Miggins' pie shop is the first place any self-respecting adventurer would head to on their way into the Murk. When Mundymutts ransack her shop and its found her secret spice mix was stolen, the party must investigate the clues and uncover a rival pie-man's jealous scheme before the Murk loses its pies for good.

#### **Starting the Adventure:**
*"Mrs Miggins' pie shop is the talk of every surrounding Shard tavern - her pies' delicious seasoning and flaky crust have sustained many a bold venture into the wilderness. Naturally, as you prepare to brave the Murk for a research expedition, you'd stop here first. But as you approach her cozy stone shop, something is clearly wrong. The door hangs ajar from its hinges and the gates to the cottage garden have been torn apart. There are signs of a scuffle inside, and as you enter cautiously, you discover three punk-themed Mundymutts ransacking the place."*

Mrs Miggins is tied up, badly injured, and uncommunicative. She requires stabilization (Physician's Kit, a Basic Potion, etc.) before she can talk. If aided, she explains she came down this morning to find the Mundymutts rooting around and is shocked to discover her secret spice mix has also been stolen - the Mundymutts know nothing about it. She begs the party to help recover the spices, offering lifetime credit at her shop, and reheats a few frozen pies as provisions for the road (one per PC, GM determines Attribute boost or randomize).

#### **Suggested Story:**
1. Set the scene of Mrs Miggins' shop, describe the ransacking, then continue.
2. **Pie Shop Pandemonium:**
	1. Three Mundymutts are ransacking the shop. They are extremely cowardly and will try to run as soon as one goes down.
	2. If captured, the Mundymutts can be easily persuaded (Negotiation with a free Minor Bonus) to reveal they were tipped off by Holg the Herbalist and told there would be no magical defenses.
	3. Mrs Miggins offhandedly mentions that Symon "the Pie Man" in town would love to get his hands on her spice formula. A search of her spice cupboard reveals a scrap of black velvet caught on the wall - certainly not belonging to any Mundymutt.
3. **Following the Trail:**
	1. The party can investigate in any order - the Alchemists Guild in town (Node 2) or Holg's dwelling in the Murk (Node 5).
	2. **Alchemists Guild:** Crawford needs persuading but his records reveal Symon was warded the same week as Miggins, still hasn't paid, and has asked for wards on a shack deeper in the forest.
	3. **Holg's Hut:** Holg has had a bad feeling about organizing the raid and is keen to make amends. He reveals Symon used him as a go-between but knows nothing about the spice mix theft itself. He can give directions to the shack.
4. **The Shack & The Showdown:**
	1. The shack is trapped with a (literal) bear trap. Past the locked door is Symon's laboratory, where the spice mix can be found.
	2. If the party recovers the spice mix and confronts Symon at his shop in Roost, he fights to silence them, peppering the battle with dramatic monologues about how Miggins stole his family's recipe generations ago.

#### **Important Characters:**
**Mrs Miggins**
*Elf, Female, ??? (Basic Folk)*
An Elf of unknown age who has been making pies for as long as anyone can remember. Going through a tattoo-punk phase of late. Blunt but poppy.
***"Bruised but bright-eyed, ink crawls up her arms and neck in floral patterns, and flour dust on her apron is similar to the chaos surrounding her."***

**Symon "The Pie Man"**
*???, Male, ??? (Werebear)*
A person of unknown species, constantly clad in regal armor under standard baker's attire. All show and no substance when it comes to actual baking.
***"The gleam of his polished breastplate is offset by the flour-dusted apron tied over it. He speaks with grand dramatic flair, gesturing with a rolling pin as though it were a scepter."***

**Holg the Herbalist**
*Gruun, Male, 60 (Mushdoom)*
An older herbalist who lives just into the Murk, occasionally trekking to Roost to exchange cautionary tales and remedies. Has no great love for adventurers but he's no fool - and he's a big fan of Miggins' pies. Does not take kindly to being pushed around.
***"Covered in moss-like patches and smelling faintly of damp earth and dried herbs, the old Gruun moves like he's got nowhere to be and knows it."***

**Crawford Ellison**
*Chib, Male, 45 (Sage)*
An uptight, posh, and studious Chib who leads the Alchemists Guild. A once-nomadic guild, now stationed in Roost as a hub for studying the Murk. Offers protective warding and attire to the townsfolk.
***"Huge spectacles sit crooked on his nose and his wispy beard has clearly not seen a comb in days. For the leader of a frontier guild, he has the frantic energy of someone expecting an audit."***

#### **Key Locations:**
**Mrs Miggins' Pie Shop**
***"A cozy stone shop from which the smell of delicious baking normally drifts, its door now hangs ajar and the cottage garden gates have been torn from their hinges."***
Despite the ransacking, the place still smells faintly of cinnamon. Flour-dusted shelves line the walls and a modest kitchen sits behind a serving counter. Freshly paid-up magical wards from the Alchemists Guild should have kept intruders out.

**Alchemists Guild**
***"A hollow bone carapace of some massive horned creature, propped up on bricks at the edge of town. Smoke puffs from a clipped horn while alchemical trinkets dangle from the other."***
Crawford needs persuading via Negotiation, boosted by offering a Booster Cake or promising contracted services (he's on a brutal deadline). If Negotiation fails, the warding roster can be found in his back office via a Deftness Check. 

If caught: Grit Check, failure results in the Ballooned condition as Crawford huffily rolls the PC out. Sells protective attire for Murk travel (Anti-Hazard Suits, Rebreathers, etc.).

**Symon's Shack**
***"An eerie, falling-down shack some ways into the deep Murk, sitting alone on an island over a shallow knee-high pond. Twisted roots reach for the canopy above."***
See Node 7 on the Adventure Map for full trap and encounter details.

**Minor Locations:**
1. **White Lion Tavern:** The local watering hole where adventurers gather before heading into the Murk. Rumors and leads flow freely here.
2. **Symon's Pie Shop:** Freshly painted and gorgeously adorned, the aroma is disappointingly stale and the meat under-seasoned. Shelves groan with unsold pies.
3. **Rezzik's Wagon Co:** A Gruun runs a wagon service for transporting goods to and from the Murk's edge. 10 Stone/ride.

#### Secrets and Clues
1. Mrs Miggins' magical wards were standard-issue from the Alchemists Guild, changed weekly - anyone warded that same week could bypass them.
2. Symon had his wards installed the same week as Mrs Miggins, and still hasn't paid for them.
3. Symon used Holg as a go-between to get the Mundymutts to ransack the shop as a cover-up, after his own men had already stolen the spice mix the night before.
4. Holg is quite fond of Mrs Miggins and didn't go himself - he sent the Murk Mutts on the condition they wouldn't hurt her.
5. Symon has asked Crawford for wards on a remote shack deeper in the forest - "a godforsaken place that surely can't be his next business venture."
6. A scrap of black velvet found in Miggins' spice cupboard certainly doesn't belong to any Mundymutt - compelling evidence that someone else was here first.
7. Mrs Miggins vaguely recalls that Symon claims she stole his family's recipe generations ago - a grudge that has driven his entire business venture.
8. Rumor around town is that Symon comes from a genuine noble line within Shard, and is simply trying to prove something to his family.
9. Holg often throws odd jobs to the Murk Mutts tribe in return for help with distracting adventurers from his more sensitive herb patches.

#### Quest Resolution
**Fighting:** If the party confronts Symon with solid proof (the recovered spice mix or the black velvet), he fights to the death before his business empire crumbles. He dramatically monologues about Miggins' dishonesty and his noble quest to reclaim his family's legacy. Town guards arrive after the fight to imprison him.

**Loot:** Symon carries 50 Coins, an Engraved Rolling Pin [Mighty Weapon, Minor Bonus on Cooking Checks], and a suit of Magnificent Heavy Armor.

**Rewards:** Mrs Miggins provides the party a lifetime pie credit, 1 Gem, and deep appreciation. Recipe back in hand, she whips up a generous amount of fresh pies for the party.

**Locations:**
1. **Mrs Miggins' Shop [Start]:**
	- The adventure's starting point. The stone building in upper Roost, currently ransacked. 
	- It is a short walk to Node 2 and Node 3 in the village.
2. **Alchemists Guild:** 
	- On the edge of town. Crawford's records reveal who else was warded that week and the location of the shack (Node 7).
3. **Symon's Pie Shop [Encounter]:**
	- In the village. The final confrontation point if the party has solid evidence. 
	- Symon [Werebear] fights here. If using the Increase option, add Gnash [Snarl].
4. **Murk Outskirts [Harmful Terrain]:**
	- Half a Day's Travel from Roost to Holg's Hut on a Path. 
	- Per Journey Check, all Players must make an Aura Check or take a level of Fatigue as the Murk's flora drains their energy. Avoided by being a Bio-Mechanoid, wearing a Rebreather, or wearing an Anti-Hazard Suit.
5. **Holg's Hut [Encounter]:**
	- An isolated, tumbledown cottage deeper into the woods. Holg is a solitary herbalist, and he's no fool.
	- Holg [Mushdoom] can be persuaded via Negotiation or subdued in Combat (surrenders at 2 Hearts or below). He reveals Symon used him as a go-between for the Mundymutt raid, but knows nothing about the spice mix theft itself. He gives directions to the shack (Node 7).
	- If any PC is Fatigued, he offers a tea remedy as a show of good faith. In exchange for a pie, he gives 2 Doses of a remedy for Jellyfication.
6. **Deep Murk [Harmful Terrain]:**
	- 1 Days' Travel from either Holg's or Roost via Trailblazing.
	- Per Journey Check, all Players must make a Grit Check or become Jellyfied as bioluminescent spores touch their skin. Avoided by being a Bio-Mechanoid or wearing an Anti-Hazard Suit.
7. **The Shack [Trap] [Guardian Encounter]:**
	- An eerie, falling-down shack on an island over a shallow, knee-high pond. Twisted roots reach for the canopy and occasional calls of some horrid beast echo from the outskirts. The door reads "Bear Trap, beware."
	- If any Player is Jellyfied, the pond counts as Harmful Terrain to them.
	- Inside, a staircase leads down to a stone-fortified hallway. A solitary, clearly visible bear trap sits in the middle of the room before a heavy locked stone door - but this is a decoy. The door can be opened with the right Gear or creative Ability usage.
	- The real trap is a pressure plate in front of the door. Insight Check (or Cautious Movement) to detect it. If not seen, a Deftness Check to approach the door without triggering it. If set off, a bear-like creature [Chompa] drops from the ceiling with a large stone key around its neck.
8. **Symon's Laboratory [Loot]:**
	- Past the locked door, Symon has set this up as a secret lab to duplicate Mrs Miggins' spice mix. Shelves are lined with herbs and spices, and different crust mixtures sit in an ice-box alongside packets of Miggins' pies.
	- Hidden in the shelves (Insight Check or relevant Abilities) is Mrs Miggins' secret mix, with the label half-peeled off.
