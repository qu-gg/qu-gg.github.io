#### **Adventure Description:** 
When a literal boatload for fiery little salamanders haul an outrageously large fish into a beachside bar and demand a feast, the party must step up and aid the tired, solo bartender in preparing a grand party while defending the catch from jealous adversaries.

#### **Starting the Adventure:** 
*"No trip in Taaga is complete without hitting up the legendary Glistening Coast, famed for shimmering waters, the best waves, and unusually large fish. In the wake of an exhausting contract, you decided to take a break and relax. Here it is you found open-air Shark Head Bar with the perfect vibes and view of the picturesque coast. Just as you get settled, however, what seems like a literal boatload of small red amphibian-folk dressed in bargain bin wizard robes step off a boat. Held aloft on dozens of tiny hands is the largest fish you've ever seen. When they demand a great feast, the solo bartender tiredly asks for your aid in the preparations."*

This is a silly light-hearted adventure and meant to accentuate a character's skill set. Be transparent about the requirements of each Feast Task. Also, consider leveraging the [Refuge in Slapstick](https://breakrpg.blogspot.com/2023/12/freebie-troublesome-trio-adversaries.html) rules which limits the Injuries that can be dealt to the Party by these adversaries.

#### **Suggested Story:**
1. Set the scene, describe Shark Head Bar, then continue.
2. **A Day to Relax?**
	1. Let the Party take the [Fishing](https://breakrpg.blogspot.com/2025/06/adventure-hooks-fishing-activity-for.html), Socialization, or Preparation Activity to start, describing how peaceful it is.
	2. Once settled, describe the procession of the Brigade carrying the fish to the bar. A feast is demanded. The tired bartender Shark asks the party to help prepare it, offering a cut of the payment.
3. **Grand Preparation:**
	1. Shark describes the 3 Tasks required to make a successful party:
		1. **Gather**: Spriki Nuts [Source, BREAK!! Blog](https://breakrpg.blogspot.com/2025/11/blazing-garden-items-freebie-gear.html) are the perfect seasoning, none else will do. Problem is their rare trees are found in remote riverbeds. He gives the Party a Grapple Shot to aid in climbing the trees [Source, BREAK!! Blog](https://breakrpg.blogspot.com/2025/11/blazing-garden-items-freebie-gear.html). Guide/Insight Check to find (or take Fatigue) and a Deftness (with Major Bonus) to get them (or Falling Injury).
		2. **Filet:** The massive fish needs to be skinned and scored to roast well. An Attack of 15 is required to properly cut the fish. Failure still finishes the job but the Attacker takes a Light Injury. Characters with the Cooking Ability may use it instead, with an Edge.
		3. **Direct:** The feast grounds must be set, and is too much for a few to handle. There are plenty of Emberclaws but their attention spans are...limited. Successful Negotiation is required to direct them, any Aptitude vs. the Emberclaws' Insight. Failure still finishes the job but the extended effort causes Fatigue.
4. **Mood Dampeners**
	1. The fire lights! However, during cooking, the Great Ribbit Coven \[1 Hopper Majo + 6 Frog Ninjas\] strikes! The party must defend the roasting fire from watery devastation.
		1. This appearance sets the Emberclaws in panic. All Battlefield Areas are Precarious for Medium or larger Species and Isolated for Small Species as dozens of small amphibians run in circles.
		2. The Hopper Majo will use **Splash Zone** every Turn on the fish-spit. 3 applications will ruin the fish!
		3. Frog Ninjas will use Defend or other defensive actions to protect the Majo.
	2. Coven defeated, the feast goes as planned. The Brigade offers to teach the group's party animal their ancient fire magicks aboard their ship.
#### **Important Characters:**
****
**Shark Head**
*Neridian, Male, 46 (Basic Folk)*
Proprietor of Shark Head Bar, running its chill vibes for most his life. Uses the fish trade system to trade with land-locked settlements farther in Taaga.
***"Despite an eye physiology that seems difficult for most land-dwelling humanoid tasks, it is surprisingly effective for keeping a simultaneous tab on customers."***

**The Flamebearer**
*Emberclaw, Male, 75 (Emberclaw Salamancer)*
Commander of The Emberclaw Brigade. Proclaims grand statements that are actually quite mundane about their conquest of the water.
***"The only way one could distinguish this Commander from the others is by the fact his little wizard hat and robe are of a slightly different color."***

**Mero Croaken the Wise**
Ribbit, Male, 80 (Hopper Majo)
Archmage of The Great Ribbit Coven. Considers the Brigade to be buffoons that make fun of the serious discipline of wizardry. Super regal and petty.
***"Dressed in the finest garbs of an ostentatious wizard, this ribbit's uptight, serious demeanor is betrayed by his visual of a cute frog in a wizard's hat."**

#### **Key Locations:**
**Shark Head Bar**
***"Welcome to Shark Head's! I'm Shark Head. Simple rules here: bring a fish, get a drink on me. Bigger fish, bigger drink."***
Characters who opt Socialize at Shark Head Bar rather than fish gain the benefit of a **Relaxing** Recreational Attraction [Source, pg. 202]. So-called Sharktails (themed cocktails) act as Basic Potions [Source, pg. 177].

**The Feast Grounds**
***"The dedicated feast grounds are easily spotted for it is a large circle of charred sand amidst the otherwise pristine beach."***
The grounds are nearby to the Shark Head Bar but tucked away around eye's view. A variety of furniture and decorations are available here, though are in varying states of being charred.

**Ship of the Brigade**
***"Despite the Brigade's goofy personality, their ship is a veritable war machine. Stocked with cutting crimson sails and a massive metal cannon, you'd be afraid were it anyone else at the helm."***
The Emberclaw Brigade spent the vast majority of their time here and, however they make it, have spent most of their funds on it. Its lower chambers are filled with ritual teaching circles.

#### Secrets and Clues
1. The Emberclaw Brigade is a nomadic tribe of fiery Salamanders that primarily live on the seas, and have built a mostly respected lineage of powerful fire magicks. 
2. They've been eternally tasked by some entity - likely a whimsical Unshaped - to wage war against water itself. So, what better battlefield than the sea? 
3. The Emberclaw Brigade primarily sticks to the Shining Sea around Taaga (due to the prevalence of massive sea life) but, on occasion, make landfall to resupply. Sometimes, however, they land to celebrate the heroic slaying of a notable sea creature. 
4. Eternal enemies of the Emberclaw Bridgade, the Great Ribbit Coven has been forever tasked with the destruction of fire itself.
5. The conflict between the Coven and Brigade is thought to be the result of a petty conflict between some two Divine Rulers claiming domain of fire and water, respectively. Despite them being gone, it seems this war wages on.
6. There is a suspicion that both of these parties are actually immortal (or incredibly incompetent) as despite constant warfare, their ranks have not diminished.
7. Shark is tired only because the Brigade does this with some frequency, causing the cook to quit the last time. He puts up with it because they pay well.

#### Quest Resolution
**Rewards:** On successful completion of the festivities, the Emberclaws will invite a party member (likely the biggest party-ier) to receive a gift via flame-dance ritual. They will impart their knowledge of **Emberclaws' Firebreath**. Shark lets the party keep the **Grapple Shot** [Source, BREAK!! Blog](https://breakrpg.blogspot.com/2025/11/blazing-garden-items-freebie-gear.html) and offers them free drinks for the week alongside 100C.

**Emberclaws' Firebreath**
*Having no written text whatsoever on their mastery of Flame Mana, the Emberclaws are quite adept at teaching verbally. It's recommended not to use this Ability in flammable spaces.*
- Once per Fight, you can attempt to breath a gout of fire at a foe up to 1 Battlefield Area away.
- **Requires a Contest**
	- Your Grit vs. their Deftness.
	-  **Success**: The shot does 2 Hearts of Flame Damage.
	- **Failure**: The shot fizzles, doing nothing.
- Adds 1 Bright Allegiance Point.
