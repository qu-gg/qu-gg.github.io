#### **Adventure Description:** 
The tranquil, jungle waters near a fishing hamlet have been invaded by a huge, aquatic monster. It is threatening the ecosystem, attacking villagers, and blocking passage to necessary local herbs. The party need to act quickly to save the river, before the creature claims its next victim.

#### **Starting the Adventure:** 
*Bruised and soaked after being boat-wrecked by that **thing**, you have finally stumbled your way to the river village of Tikuto. World-renowned for Pheeka's Fishing Shop, it is known as a prosperous and lively fishing village built on stilts and walkways. This seems far from accurate, however, as the melancholic faces and dour mood physically washes over you. No coracles are in the water, nor any isle fisherman or playful folk. You know why for you've seen it yourself - a monster is in the waves.*

A vicious Deadwitch has moved into the waters near the usually happy fishing village of Tikuto, sat along the Deynali River in the Sol Alliance. She has begun manipulating the ecosystem for her vile experiments, attacking fishers, and worse yet, has taken up residence on a nearby island that has much needed restorative herbs. The village has run out of supplies and larger boats, the injured are stuck in pain, including the increasing number of waylaid travelers. The town guardian named Bressa had been tasked with at the least driving away the monster, but was seriously wounded in her attempt. Use the BREAK!! Fishing [rules](https://breakrpg.blogspot.com/2025/06/adventure-hooks-fishing-activity-for.html).

#### **Suggested Story:**
1. Describe the party stumble into town, evaluate the village, have Bressa limp into the party's path, then continue:
2. **Fishing for Help:**
	1. Bressa recounts, and confirms, the village's trouble, and offers substantial reward by the village elder for those who help.
	2. Once the party agree to help, Bressa shares her proposed next plan. She hopes to repair a larger fishing vessel, place bait into the water, then ambush it.
3. **Sink or Swim:**
	1. To repair a boat, the party will need to ask Talki permission to use reeds from a nearby magical thicket. It has been defaced with decaying fish corpses and claw marks against the shrines.
	2. Depending on the quality of repairs, the boat will have 1, 2, or 4 Hearts. It has a Defense Rating of 12.
4. **Barrels of Bait:**
	1. The party need to learn what would be the best bait; Bressa directs them to Phreeka, the famed storekeeper.
	2. She advises fishing for the rare razormouth on a nearby jetty. Results of fishing are:
		1. Catching a razormouth will enable the party to Ambush the Deadwitch who is transfixed on it
		2. Any non-common fish will cause it reveal itself on accident, starting a normal Combat Start
		3. No fish will cause the Deadwitch to Ambush
5. **Briny Brawl:**
	1. When the party is ready for the showdown, Bressa gives them directions to the herbal island and wishes them luck. Combat starts depending on the bait's quality. The Deadwitch attacks the boat first, then the party once they're in the water.
	2. The Deadwitch will look to flee, or hide and regenerate, at 1 Heart. Regardless of it fleeing or being slain, Tikuto will celebrate and prepare what feast they can.

#### **Important Characters:**
**Brassa**
*Promethean, Female, 31 (Custrel)*
Everyone in town sees her as its protector and undisputed best fisher. Though generally well-meaning, she is loud and aggressive to a fault.
***“Towering high above most in the village, this proud Promethean is adorned in armor made from various fish scales. It kinda smells, but no one dares tell her.”***

**Speaker Talki**
*Human, Female, 74 (Scholar)*
Tikuto's spiritual leader, and the one who handles all communication with Sol Alliance officials. Folks look to her in matters of conflict resolution.
***"Despite a hunch and wiry grey hair from a lifetime of solving other's problems, this gentle older woman has laugh lines of a life well-lived."***

**Pheeka**
*Undead, Female, ??? (Undead Peddlar)*
Owner of the world-reknowned Pheeka's Fishing Shop, she is actually a well-preserved Undead Peddlar. Oldest member of Tikuto *by far*.
**“A wide, knowing smirk is set across this eccentric old lady's face as the seashells in her shop's doorway, and her hair, clunk each other.”**

**Deadwitch:** 
The Deadwitch is a reflavored Drakkenheim Sewer Gator, having the same Aptitudes and Abilities. However, in addition, it has an Edge on Attacks and Might Checks against Targets in Water.
**"The lifetime of a Sea Witch is quite long, but even then, sometimes their lives are ended prior to their goals being realized. Given their domain, it is not a large step to undeath for these Witches, temporary as it is. All negotiable or scheming nature disappears, regressing into an obsessive monster for their pursuits."**
#### **Key Locations:**
**Pheeka's Fishing Shop**
***"At the edge of town and so close to the Deynali it's nearly falling in, this is the premier shop for fishing supplies. Technically it's the only fishing shop, but still, it is impressive."***
An Undead Peddlar Pheeka runs this shop and has for a long, long time. She keeps her ear to the ground and is a good source of information for adventurers she likes. Her Inventory is on the [blogpost](https://breakrpg.blogspot.com/2025/12/river-village-tikuto-freebie-settlement.html).

**Belu Thicket***
***"A serene and tranquil grove set down a stream from Tikuto, several ancient statues to a river god have been brutally defaced here."***
Besides holding shrines to Belugosh, the ancient river god, this place is also home to a collection of [Strengthened Scroll Reeds](https://breakrpg.blogspot.com/2025/11/blazing-garden-items-freebie-gear.html), magically-infused bright blue reeds. The Deadwitch defaced this place.

**The Deynali River**
***"Traversing back up the river you came, the gentle waters are unsettling in their silence. Reflections of the sun make it hard to see through the waves."***
Halfway to the island of herbs, the party may set up the bait and lie in wait, result of which depends on their fishing success. Create a suspenseful atmosphere against the beautiful surroundings.
#### Secrets and Clues
1. The ribbit motif throughout Tikuto and the Deynali river is deference to an ancient river god named Belugosh, said to take on any aquatic form. Fishers from far and wide come here for a chance to meet her.
2. The Deadwitch was named Afumi in life and was focused on findings ways to displace gods in their domains. When she was getting close to that goal, Belugosh put an end to her...or so Belugosh thought. Now, Afumi's goal is displacing *her* specifically.
3. All of Tikuto's sturdier boats were destroyed by the monster, the ones stemming from the thicket's wood.
4. Belugosh is known to appear to and reward those who manage to catch a razormouth in her waters. As such, they are very valuable to the Deadwitch.
5. It is common for villagers to give Talki gifts and mementos after seeking her insight, many seeing her as Tikuto's grandmother.
6. The Belu Thicket a sacred site holding shrines to Belugosh known for its extra-buoyant wood.
7. Pheeka has some dusty old tomes on Deadwitches, indicating their singular focus in death and indiscriminate use of ambush tactics in their waters.
8. The Deadwitch only assaulted the village itself once, raiding a fish storage shack. Oddly, the only thing it took was a prized razormouth fish.

#### Quest Resolution
**Rewards:** Talki will let the party keep the repaired **Felucca** [Source, pg. 198], offering to help retrieve and repair it if it was sunk. As well, she'll offer 2 baskets of **Pini Beans** [Trade Goods. Source, pg. 179].

The village will throw a great feast, and the party will have free **Pleasant Room & Hearty Meals** [Source, pg. 201] for the duration of their stay.
