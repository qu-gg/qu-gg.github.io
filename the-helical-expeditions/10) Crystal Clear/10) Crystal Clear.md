#### **Adventure Description:** 
The party venture to the crystalline fields of Crystalia to find a stubborn benefactor of the Archive who has been gone for too long. As a series of abandoned campsites are discovered, it becomes clear that there may be more to this missing person case than first meets the eye.

#### **Starting the Adventure:** 
*"After no shortage of odd sea encounters and arcane disturbances, you finally reach the shores of Crystalia near the White Moth Opera House. This is the last known location of the stubborn Shard Patrician, and your Helical benefactor, Halowen Sool. You breathe a frigid sigh of relief into the glimmering air as you see signs of the landing hub set against a cliff's base ahead. But, as you approach, despair creeps in as it's clear this area has been abandoned for some time."*

Halowen is a powerful Shard Patrician who has helped fund the Helical Research Archive for the better part of his long Elven life. Enthralled with "the daring unknown", he's lived vicariously through the Archive until an opportunity was presented by a local Adventuring Guild to give him a tour of The White Moth Opera House in Crystalia...for a hefty, hefty price. Despite the protest by both the Archive, who wanted to scout it out first, and his cabinet, he sneakily set off with this Guild. The Helical Archive has contracted the party, a few days later, to pursue him and ensure his safety.

A few days ago, Doppelgangers killed and replaced his hired guides. They are now leading him to their nest as food for their children. Require Grit Checks (or appropriate Gear) per Day against the Cold or characters take levels of Fatigue. To add tension, add a strict time limit to this adventure, forcing the party to Rush instead of Camping if they get Lost. 

#### **Suggested Story:**
1. Set the scene of Crystalia, describe the landing hub, then continue.
2. **A Gory Clue:**
	1. Nearby the party finds a bloody trail leading off behind some frosted trees. Lookout's body has been scavenged by 3 Crystalline Dregs, who will attack.
3. **Campsite to Campsite:**
	1. Footprints lead through the Prismatic Wasteland instead of to the Opera House.
	2. Sap's body can be found put inside a hollow crystal. He is half-eaten.
4. **Doppel Trouble**
	1. Halowen and the Doppelgangers can be found in a relaxed conversation at the Opera Overlook campsite.
	2. If the party mention Halowen’s safety, or the bodies they found, the Doppelgangers will quarrel with them and defend themselves. If pressed, they will attempt to Ambush, surprising both Halowen and the party.
	3. With the Doppelgangers defeated, the party can descend back to the Landing Hub with Halowen, if he survived.

#### **Important Characters:**
**Halowen Sool**
*Elf, Male, 134 (Basic Folk)*
This wealthy explorer wishes to tour Crystalia with the help of his two trusted guides.
***"Deswin tries to take pride in his appearance, but harsh winds have blown the auburn toupée upon his head into a scruffy mess"***

**Lookout Beello**
*Gruun, Female, 34 (Doppelganger)*
This Doppelganger uses Lookout’s influence as the lead guide to deceive and manipulate Halowen.
***“Most of Lookout’s features are obscured by the hooded, fur-trimmed coat that keeps her warm. She stands upright with assertive confidence.”***

**Sap Hire**
*Dwarf, Male, 26 (Doppelganger)*
Disguised as the younger and eager guide, this Doppelganger tries to follow all of Lookout and Halowen’s requests.
***“Belts around this stocky dwarf’s body hold satchels, climbing tools and coils of rope.”***

#### **Key Locations:**
**Landing Hub**
***"Just off the icy shore, a relatively sheltered site with some makeshift huts sits at the base of a cliff, right next to a shimmering tunnel."***
A Doppelganger ambushed Lookout when she was looking for firewood off-site and shapeshifted into her. Its claws left bloody prints in the snow.

Recent footprints in the crystal dust lead inwards on a dedicated path through crystalline tunnels.

**Prismatic Campsite**
***"The trail leads off the obvious route into a dense crystal wasteland instead, eventually ending with another campsite in a broken crystal alcove."***
The Doppelganger posing as Lookout killed Sap in the night; its mate shapeshifted to replace him. Displaced crystals suggest the group went farther up the ridge around the Opera House.

**Opera Overlook**
***"Walking along the edge of the ridge, a beautiful view of the Opera House is held. Occasionally the haunting interior can be seen through cracks in its walls."***
Halowen and the Doppelgangers’ moods are upbeat and jolly. The fake guides tell Halowen they are making good progress.

If possible, the Doppelgangers will try to sway the party to leave.

#### Secrets and Clues
1. Despite the Adventuring Guild's scouting of this place, the presence of Doppelgangers is not widely known in Crystalia.
2. The Doppelgangers' brood of children live in a cave a Day's travel from the Opera Overlook.
3. Since meeting Halowen, they have been looking after him and feeding him very well.
4. Humanoid-sized bites can be found on Lookout's body that don't quite match the larger Dreg bites.
5. It is difficult to discern identifying features on the bodies, due to their conditions and frost damage.
6. The Doppelgangers convinced Halowen to take a path to the back entrance instead due to a Urarani sighting on the main walkway.
7. A journal in Sap's satchel refers to a change in Lookout's attitude after leaving the base campsite.
8. Halowen's group stayed at the landing hub for a few days to acclimate to the mana flux here, and  avoid Arcane Contamination Syndrome farther in.

#### White Moth Fields [Adventure Map]
![[Pasted image 20260104122226.jpg]]
> Map of Crystalia near the White Moth Opera House. Art by Inkarnate.

**Locations:**
1. **Expedition Landing Hub**: Comprised of a few permanent-standing huts, this is where the Adventurer's Guild lands to acclimate to Crystalia's mana flux before heading inwards. A recent trail of footsteps leads into the reflective crystalline tunnel nearby. 
	1. It is half a Day's Travel on a Path to Node 2.
2. **A Curious Fork:**  Exiting the tunnel, the party sees a long gossamer walkway leading to a gleaming ivory tower, suspending in a crumbling state.
	1. Despite that the expedition's target, they distinctly see the trail go off-course into a rising crystal field nearby.
	2. The party can choose to follow the walkway (Road, Node 3) or continue on the trail (Trailblazing, Node 4). Each require a Half-Day's Travel.
3. **Opera House Entrance \[Guardian Encounter\]:** Upon this cracked reflective walkway, the party sees ancient statues of gorgeous figures in dramatic poses. Upon the path, however, they encounter an Urarani who is rampaging in frustration that it cannot destroy the tower. It sets its sight on the party, instead.
4. **Prismatic Wasteland \[Harmful Terrain]:**  In this sloped jungle of jagged crystals, a powerful, ancient ritual was released here, its energies over time warping the area to a [Prismatic Wasteland](https://breakrpg.blogspot.com/2025/01/freebie-harmful-terrain.html). At its exit, another campsite is found. Here, Sap was killed during their rest and replaced with another Doppelganger. 
	- Anti-Hazard Suit required to prevent [Arcane Contamination Syndrome](https://breakrpg.blogspot.com/2024/11/freebie-arcane-contamination-syndrom.html) as they didn't have time to acclimate.
	- Sap's body can be found set into a hollow crystal nearby, half-eaten.
5. **Opera Overlook:** About halfway up the tower, this cliff-side provides a gorgeous view of it, even being able to peer inside its haunting interior through a broken wall.
	- Halowen and his guides are setup here with 3 tents, happily chatting as they wait for a weird creature on a spit to finish cooking.
	- The doppelgangers will try to sway the party to leave.
