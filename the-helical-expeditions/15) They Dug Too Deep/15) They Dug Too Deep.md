#### **Adventure Description:** 
The excavation site of an ancient Calian temple by an Archive professor within the dangerous deadlands of the Hollow Queen's Kingdom failed to check-in on time. Concerned, the Helical Archive has commissioned the party to investigate the missing crew...and find something sinister lurking within.

#### **Starting the Adventure:** 
*"A trip to the Hollow Queen's Kingdom is no peaceful feat, and touching down the airship on a small ledge in the desolate Red Eye Chasm doesn't help your anxiety either. The persistent groans of the Queen's "Children" lay ever on the outskirts of your hearing as unnatural winds chill you. At the landing site, you spot your quarry - an excavation site  said to contain ancient Calian texts. Petitioned for research by the Helical researcher Galahad Gallant, you were sent in when the agreed upon check-in date was missed. You see no signs of the contracted mining team, and to make matters worse, the front entrance appears to be caved in. Best find a way in as it is sure not safe outside."*

Given the depth of the Adventure Site and the number of rooms, this adventure is likely to go beyond a one-shot if not paced fast. This is a great introduction to Exploration mechanics and a bit of the OSR roots BREAK!! has. The interior is entirely dark so emphasis sources of light. 

Given the Shadow Blight nature of the Creature, it can be likely for a PC or two to contract it...which naturally leads to a follow-up adventure!

#### Creature
**Description:** The fleshy and shadowy demon-like creature is always hungry. It has poor vision but can track the scent of blood. Use Adventure Site Wandering Encounter Rules [Source, pg. 236] for when to check if the Creature attacks, its chance depending where the Party is. If misled, it camouflages itself in the darkness and disappears. If defeated, it will come back after exploring 2 Areas. It can only be finished if the Bloody Tome is destroyed

Creature Attack Chance \[D20]:
- Inside the mine: 18-20
- The temple: 15-20
- Master's room: 10-20. 

**Abilities:**
- +1 Heart taken from Bright Damage
- -1 Heart taken from Dark Damage
- Every instance of damage caused by the creature converts to 1 Heart of Healing
- Dual Wields Standard Weapons (Claws)
- Has Shadow Blight Host 
- **Stats:** +4 Attack | 2 Hearts  |12 Defense Rating | M8 D11 G9 I9 A11
#### Quest Resolution
**Rewards:** If the party successfully neutralize the Creature and Bloody Tome, as well as reveal the fate of the excavation, the Archive will reward the party with 200C and let them keep any items found within.

#### The Mine [Adventure Map]

**Locations:**
1. **Mine Entrance:**  [Entrance] The entrance was blown up from the inside. Leaning on a rock is the dead professor, charred from an explosion. Journal on their body starts with disappointment before remarking a "potential breakthrough." An elevator leads to the lower floor and can hold a total of 30 Slots of weight (risk of crashing if overloaded, causing Falling Injury).
2. **Abandoned Mine:** [Encounter] Scattered 2d6 dwarf corpses, all with rotting moss and cracked forms. If disturbed, they rise as Demons and attack the party (3 Zones: Party | 1d6 Corpses | 1d6 Corpses). A message hurriedly scrawled on the wall, in Under Warble: "GET OUT. BEWARE THE..."
3. **Miner's Workshop:** 2 Boxes containing: 2 Dynamite [Grenade], 2 Lanterns, 2 Units of Fuel.
4. **Dormitory:** Small chests containing: 1d6 Standard Weapons, a Small Shield, 2d20 Coins.
5. **Secret Vault:** Behind the secret passage: 4d20 gold coins, 3 Barrels of Beer [Trade Goods] and Distilled Axe [Mighty Weapon, Magical and +1 ATK when the user is Drunk].
6. **Bizzer Nest:** [Encounter] Room with small tunnels on the walls: 2 Bizzer Swarms and 3 dwarf bodies with 1d4 Units of Shade Iron. The entrances are blocked.
7. **Tumbled Cart:** Inside the cart: 1 Dynamite [Grenade]. On the floor, a message in Under Warble: "IT COMES BACK"
8. **Mining Area:** An uneven lower area with Shade Iron encrusted at the rocky floor. Mining here (as a Location Action), grants 1d4 Units.
9. **Underground Lake:** All the Areas in here are Precarious, requiring Deftness Checks to Move or become Toppled. 1 old raft in good condition near the lake.
10. **Mine Exit:** [Entrance] [Encounter] Very deep underground lake: where does it lead? 2 Water Chompas prevent the exit.
11. **Dwarf Ghosts:** These ghost dwarves think they are alive and awaiting rescue. They treat adventurers as trapped fellow dwarves and know about the creature.
12. **Unstable Area:** [Trap] Loud noises cause Falling Debris (CLICK!). Bodies of dwarves in skin and bone scattered around the room.
13. **Temple Main Hall:** Stone statues holding 4 decorative amethyst weapons (worth 25C each).
14. **Statue of the Master:** Amethyst Statue [Gargoyle, Rank 3]: becomes animated and attacks when someone approaches. Yields as sundered Magnificent Heavy Armor.
15. **Old Entrance Hall:** [Entrance] Original entrance to the temple. 2 amethyst idols on pedestals show cultists cutting the palm of their own hands: putting blood on the wall between the idols opens a secret passage out of the dungeon.
16. **Ritual Room:** Dead bodies of dwarves and cultists lay near a strange symbol drawn on the ground. On the floor, there are 2 Bright-Damage Concealed Weapons and the Bloody Tome (with the symbol on its cover). The Tome [Forgotten Lore] is sinister notes about Fleshwarping experiments conducted by a wicked Sage after Calian's fall. Has knowledge on how to summon blood-sucking creatures through blood sacrifices, as well as transferring life essence into magic materials to enhance crafts.
17. **Dining Room:** Refined and ancient furniture: bodies of blood sucked cultists seated at the table.
18. **Master's Room:** Corpse dressed in a wizard's robe. Bloodstone Staff and Lenin Obscurity Shades. Shelves with anatomy books and bottles of blood (from humans and other creatures). The closet has a false back, connecting the room to a false wall at the corridor.
