#### **Adventure Description:** 
When a blanket of grey, draining all color it touches, starts to leak out from an ancient Calian crypt, hurried cries for help ring out from the nearest Night Haven village to the ears of the party.

#### **Starting the Adventure:** 
*"Traveling to an artificial island made entirely of rusted Shade Iron and crawling red roses, you didn't really expect the 'expanding Grey'  the village-folk to be all that dreary in comparison. Your expectations are shattered, however, as you cross the threshold into it and everything becomes gray-scale and drab, including you. You were told that the likely source of this phenomena is the old crypt of a Calian Sage uncovered before you, cracked stairs leading into the earth. Rumors that one of Night Haven's immortal nobles took up residence here, for reasons publicly unknown, so not even the bravest villagers dared check inside. This is where you come in.*

This scenario acts as one big puzzle dungeon and Adventure Site, where the party must figure out how to return the color to the crypt by opening the three **Color Caskets**, which completes the goal of "stopping the expanding Grey". Only gray-tones exist in this crypt due to the Dusty Miller's actions. Anything brought in is affected as well. **Lanterns** are the only source of color.

This site presents many different entities with their own goals, and many if not all can be dealt with via Negotiation rather than Combat. There are many fun, potential interactions between the denizens of the crypt themselves (e.g., Minotaurs x the Dark-Webber, Mirrorfolk x The Dusty Miller, etc.), so encourage it if possible! Follow BREAK!!'s Exploration rules (Source, pg. 230) for how to navigate the crypt and when to roll Random Events. 
#### Secrets and Clues:
- Crypt used to be the experimentation site of a Calian Sage who was dedicated to combining the art of Fleshwarping with color stimuli.
- The Mirrorfolk were the first successful experiment of the Sage, who was quite egotistical and wanted a wave of compliments every morning.
- The Lalka were a more serious experiment in attempting lifeforms capable of warping their forms based on light. The Sage often preferred their Green variant, for reasons they didn't even understand.
- Having the unfortunate life of being born on Night Haven where the color Red permeates the entire island, these Minotaurs have found a safe haven in this grey crypt. The Alpha Minotaur has taken on the noble cause of hiding the Red Lantern from the others.
- The Dark-Webber has grand ambitions to figure out the secrets of this crypt and leverage the power of the Prism for herself, however never really gets around to acting on said ambitions.
- The Dusty Miller is a disgraced noble of Night Haven, whose power is to drain color from his environment. This, alongside his drab fashion sense, led the other more colorful nobles (e.g., the Duke of Red Roses or the Lady of Lavender) to excommunicate him from their social gatherings.
	- During exile, the Miller uncovered this crypt and, seeing its experiments with color, snapped. He imbued some caskets with his ability to drain color and trapped each of the base light colors. 
	- To the Miller's frustration, he hasn't been able to stop the Lanterns from shining light and, unbeknownst to him, his continued attempts are causing the Grey to spill out from the crypt.
	- Of the Night Haven nobles, the Dusty Miller is actually quite docile. He has hidden away in this crypt where he can be enveloped in pure grays and not be bothered by anyone.

**Locations:**
1. Entrance [Lantern] : 
	- Smells lovely. Grey clovers cover the ground. A **Green Lantern** beams light from the corner. Every clover hit turns green. The Lantern itself is immovable.
2. Mirrorfolk Quarters [Puzzle Key] : 
	- A set of Mirrorfolk chatter and laugh, playing games. They've built a simple habitat for themselves, which is kept very spic and span. 
	- Mirrorfolk [Crystalline Dreg] :
		- Happy. Flailing Limbs. Very Reflective.
		- Breaking one gives the culprit bad luck (= Snag) for a Day.
		- Each desire a different present before helping anyone. 
		- Roll 2D20 to determine what they want:
			- 1-5 | A Poem About: | Rain
			- 6-10 | The Sound of: | Thunder
			- 11-15 | To Reflect: | Death
			- 16-20 | Be Touched By: A Leprechaun
3. Webbed Intersection [Guardian Encounter] : A completely webbed intersection which the Dark-Webber guards and plots, chewing on food.
	- Moving through here without the Dark-Webber's permission requires a Deftness Check or become Restrained in the webs.
	- Dark Webber [Dizzy Slither] : 
		- Soft-spoken. Shadowy. Weaving her plots.
		- Spins webs that absorb all color.
		- Desires the light from someone's eyes, which would make them see in grayscale.
4. Ashy Room [Lantern] : 
	- A **Blue Lantern** colors the room. 6 Downer Lalkas sit around moping or half-heartedly try to escape. **Rubite** portcullises trap them in at both entrances.
	- Downer Lalka [Delinquent Goon] : 
		- Mopey. Passive-Aggressive. Vulgar. Ticked-Off.
		- Wear a blue, drooping clover on their hats.
		- Transform into a Good-Luck Lalka when exposed to **Green** Light
	- Good-Luck Lalka [Delinquent Goon] :
		- Friendly. Dancing. High-pitched Voices.
		- Transform into an Ash Gnome when not in **Green** Light.
		- Bestows a Blessing that gives an Edge on the next Check/Contest.
		- Know how to create the **Rainbow** Bridge at the **Prism Hall**
5. Prism Hall [Puzzle Key] :
	- Main grand hall with an immovable Prism. Shining **Red**, **Green**, and **Blue** light on it creates a Rainbow bridge to the crypt.
	- The Dusty Miller flies around the chasm near it, taunting anyone he meets.
	- The Dusty Miller [Count Algebra] :
		- Flying. Vain. Acts like he doesn’t care.
		- Carries the **Red** Casket with him.
		- Can only be destroyed if hit by a combination of **Red**, **Green** and **Blue** (= white) light. If defeated in Combat, he'll disappear into the grey before reappearing later.
6. Burial Crypt [Loot] :
	1. Burial site of the long passed Sage whose home this was. Cold and dusty now.
	2. 200 Grey Coins and a small **Green Casket** are here.
7. Minotaur Den [Lantern] [Guardian Encounter] : 
	- Dirty; hay everywhere causing the Area to be **Precarious**. 
	- A balcony surveys the den. 
	- A single, Alpha Minotaur [Were-Bear] keeps his **Red Lantern** close, covering its light to prevent hostilities. Change the Were-Bear's Bear-Zerker ability to activate on seeing **Red** instead.
	- Minotaurs [Yeeti] :
		- Powerful. Emotional and anxious. Breathing heavily.
		- Hostile to anything colored **Red**.
8. Treasure Rooms [Loot] :
	- Each hold 50 **Grey Coins**. The third one holds **Dusty Miller's Rapier**.
	- Dusty Miller's Rapier:
		- *An exceptionally well-made sword, forged of Shade Iron and exquisitely designed to match the sensibilities befitting a noble such as himself.*
		- A Combination Master/Quick Weapon made from Shade Iron. 
		- While the blade itself has no magical abilities, if a Forger Imbues it with anything darkness-themed (Dark Damage, causing Blindness, etc) they gain an Edge on their Crafting Check due to the Dark mana it has absorbed while in his possession. The GM is final arbiter of what counts as darkness-themed.
		- 2 Slots | 400 Coins

#### Random Encounters [D20] :
1. **1-8**: 1d4 Minotaurs, chained together, demand everyone empty their pockets and give anything **Red**.
2. **9-10**: The Miller coughs, opening his Casket for an instant, returning **Red** to the dungeon for a second.
3. **11-12**: 1d6 Ash Lalka try to open a Blue Casket (Deftness Check of 4), locked by The Miller 
4. **13-20**: The Miller messes with attempts the party makes to direct the **Lanterns'** beams.

#### Colorful Item Descriptions
**Lantern:** Beams a specific color in all four orthogonal directions.
**Rubite:** A metal that magically disappears when it’s exposed to pure **Red** light. It reappears when exposed to **Blue** light.
**Grey Coin:** Heavy and Bulky to carry, taking up a full Slot per 100 Coins. Regains its worth when it’s exposed to **Red** and **Green** (= yellow) light.
**Casket:** Rat-sized. Traps light of a specific color. When opened, everyone within an Area must make a Grit Check or have their skin turn into that color. The light will then return. When closed, it erases that color of light from your current location.
