**Of Cog's Finest Gadgeteer**
- Adaptation of Secrets of the Smith by onepagedungeons: https://1pagedungeons.itch.io/urban-crawl
- CC-BY-SA
- One of Cog's finest gadgeteers, known to all on Stahlfeld, can take any Gleysian scrap and make it a re-functioning marvel...for a hefty price. They are often a supplier to the Helical Archive, though the shop's outputs has recently taken a sharp decline in quality. 
- The Helical Archive has been waiting on time-sensitive orders for a while and has been left empty-handed or disappointed, so they send the party in to scope out the situation and find the reason.
- Leverages Bio-Mechanoid cores (claiming to only use irreparable ones) as the puretech source of his work and Drones - who seem far above the quality of other repaired ones in Cog.

Cog - Stahlfeld - Twilight Meridian

Source:
Custrel: Source, pg. 
Autodrones: Reflavored Drones, Source, pg. 
Kidnappers: Reflavored Peacekeepers that use Gleysian tech, by Quagg [Homebrew]
Scout Drones by Quagg [Homebrew]

Secrets & Clues:
1. The secret of Cormory's craft is the direct use of Gleysian Bio-Mechanoid "soul cores" in his repairs, capable of imbuing a base level of consciousness to make these ancient devices work again. 
2. Cormory doesn't know the specifics of how the imbuement truly works; he knows it works and no questions need be asked.
3. If the secret is revealed and Cormory is confronted about it, he'll become defensive and upset, claiming he only uses irreparable cores and that he has an agreement with the Archive. This is a half-truth as it's via unofficial channels with a specific researcher.
4. Cormory is planning to only share this knowledge of the craft with his first-born son, Sjort, and is careful not to share secrets with his second, Zword.
5. Brother Zword, as second-born, won't inherit the forge or its knowledge. Envy towards Sjort drove him to hire Kidnappers to take his "stolen" inheritance by force. He speaks to them via the XX Chokers.
6. The Kidnappers are a gang of unsuccessful junkers seeking to take the knowledge of Cormory's craft and setup a competitor in Sprocket. With Cormory holding out so long, their patience is running thin and they are considering robbing the workshop empty instead.
7. Both Sjort and Zword were away at the time of Cormory's disappearance, at Zword's behest.

Location:
1. **Storefront** [Guardian Encounter] :
	1. No customers inside. Half-empty racks of minor arcane devices (i.e., a Techatiel Build-Kit and some XX Chokers). Bit of bloodsplatter on the floor. 2 Kidnappers pretend to keep shop.
2. **Sleeping Quarters** [Guardian Encounter] :
	1. Messy beds, as if recently searched. 2 Kidnappers whisper about the state of plans with Zword. The closet holds a secret door.
	2. 2 Extreme Heat Outfits (Source, pg. ) within the room.
3. **Foundry**: 
	1. Hot. Constant buzzing and puffing. Low Pipes hinder movement (all Areas here are Cramped) for anyone but Small Species. 
	2. The Area is protected by 4 Scout Drones hovering in the corners. They can perceive the intentions of humanoids and will subdue anyone wishing others harm.
	3. Unlike the more rigid repurposed Drones in Cog, the "Autodrones" of the workshop seem a bit more intelligent, working away on repairs autonomously.
4. **Compactor** [Harmful Terrain] :
	1. Loud Grinding of the Compactor - 2 Areas wide or 15ft across. Powered by the Waterwheel. Crushes everything.
	2. Failing an attempt to go across results in escalating Injuries, starting with a Light Injury and increasing 1 Stage per Turn. Requires appropriate use of an Item/Ability or a successful Check (at a Snag unless an external party aids) to escape.
5. **Hearth** [Harmful Terrain] : 
	1. Zone full of flames, heating Pipes and metal within. There's a door behind the flames to Cormory's Secret Office.
	2. Exposure to the flames directly results in a Serious Burn (Source, pg. 267) per Turn.
6. **Waterwheel** [Guardian Encounter] :
	1. Gadgeteer Cormory is bound to the waterwheel itself, a stream of water constantly turning him. 3 bored Kidnappers hold guard, playing games.
	2. A door hides behind the water-powered gears, leading to Cormory's Secret Office.
	3. Gadgeteer Cormory   [Custrel]
		- Drowsy. Soft-spoken. Full prosthetic torso and arms. 
		- Knows how to build/fix Autodrones. 
		- Knows how to repair very complex Gleysian relics. 
		- Unwilling to share this knowledge. 
7. **Secret Office** [Loot] : 
	1. 2 Drones protect schematics on a desk. These papers hold the secrets to the use of Bio-Mechanoid Cores in Cormory's work.
	2. A hatch can open a pipe ending here.

Random Events:
	1-4: The Kidnappers in the room become Hostile, attempting an Ambush.
	5-9: 2 Autodrones short-circuit, making them attack the party.
	10-13: Sjort & Zword argue about the disappearance of Cormory, forcing the party to take a side.
	14-16: A faint trail of bloodsplatters points in the direction of the Waterwheel.
	17-18: Zword assures everyone his dad has gone on a spontaneous vacation and not to worry.
	19-20: Sjort is asking around if anyone has seen his dad recently.
