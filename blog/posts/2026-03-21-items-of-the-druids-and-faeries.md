As mentioned in the last [session report](http://its.quagg.studio/blog/posts-html/2026-03-19-session-report-38-break-fight-against-urarani.html), I was interested in some spirits/druidic items the party might find as loot in the now uncorrupted ruins. I often like to give out rewards that interact or augment specific mechanics/procedures the party might be lacking in while not bypassing or solving it outright. So, I found some inspiration and thought up some stuff. Here are a few items! Happy to take thoughts and feedback on 'em.

### Everbark Figurine
This item was a fun one to build out, and inspiration credit goes to my [rootring](https://rootr.ing/) neighbor [d4caltrops](https://blog.d4caltrops.com/) (big fan!) from their [Magical Toys & Trinkets](https://blog.d4caltrops.com/2026/01/d100-magical-toys-trinkets.html) post. It's a fairly subtle boost but could make a huge difference for a party that can't afford to take Fatigue. Plus, it gave me some thoughts on how to flesh Elix out as a character: her having been a part of a nomadic druid circle after the Sundering who aimed to heal the land and its peoples where they could before she eventually settled down in the Spirit's Grove.
:::item
name: Everbark Figurine
type: Trinket
desc: When, at journey's end, Ever-Walking Elix bid farewell to her best friend, she carved fond \nmemories into wood until her body simply said 'I think we've gone far enough.'
effect:
- Once per Journey, during Camping, this little figurine can be activated to become a Growl made of Warp Root.
- This Growl functions as a Scout, and has an Edge on the associated Deftness Check.
stats: 0.1 Slot . 50 Coins
:::

### Fellowmark Charms
Granting restricted use of Quirks (or Calling Abilities!) via Magic Items can be a fun and easy means of creating rewards in BREAK!!, as evidenced by [Legacy Gems](https://breakrpg.blogspot.com/2026/02/legacy-gems-freebie.html) on the official blog. I like the angle that the item is actually a gift from the creature kingdoms to the Druids rather than being arcana in nature.

:::item
name: Fellowmark Charms
type: Trinket
desc: They say giving a piece of yourself to another lets them understand you better. For Druids, this is a quite literal saying.
effect: 
- Once per Day, the Charm may bestow the Wearer the Beast Tongue Quirk (Source, pg. 141) for an Hour.
- The Charm's token specifies which creature category it enables speech with (e.g., a feather for birds)
- Activating the Quirk's Disadvantage destroys the item and invokes a roll on the Severe Injury Table, as a \npowerful promise was broken.

stats: 0.5 Slot . 50 Coins
:::

### Bag o' Feathers
Admittedly, this is homebrew I already made in the past and posted on Discord but it is relevant to the theme. Messing with Inventory Slots is a devious trick and this one really hurts (ask my one player). It hurts doubly so if it's from a glib faerie offering "help" while you're in a sinking mud pit.
:::item
name: Bag o' Feathers
type: Cursed Artifact
desc: Any faerie that believes itself disrespected (and often it will believe this, seemingly over nothing) \n will offer a Bag of Feathers as a "boon", claiming it enables one to fly.
effect: Anyone who takes this gift will unfortunately realize the bag is extremely burdensome, \nand certainly the heaviest feathers in the world.
- It can only be removed from one's Inventory if they can pass it off to a willing Creature.
- It immediately takes up 3 Inventory Slots.
stats: 3 Slots . ???
:::

### Grass Sword
Straight outta Adventure Time on this one and a fairly by-the-book weapon, just with the addition of the Edge on the Ambush and it being weightless. Despite the Jealous Curse, this one is pretty good! It even has an open Weapon Ability slot to add some extra juice to it.

:::item
name: Grass Sword
type: Cursed Artifact
desc: Often sold by Hedge Mages, these blades are surprisingly deft and precise \ndespite their flimsy appearance. Good luck breaking up with it, though. 
effect: The sword is a Concealed/Master Weapon made out of Warp Root.
- It is Cursed with Jealousy (Source, pg. 295).
- However, it grants the Wielder an Edge on their Ambush Contest for Concealed Weapons' Sneaky Swipe ability.
- As it is made of grass and wraps around the Wielder's arm, it takes up no Inventory Slots.
stats: 0 Slot . 1 Gem
:::
