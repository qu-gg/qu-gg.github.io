Hi there! 

I'm Quagg, and welcome to my TTRPG blog (or rather the start of it). I have little plans for it besides showcasing new homebrew or adventures I write, highlighting hobby things I like, and play-reports for me to look back at over time.

### Background on me:
I've been a TTRPG enjoyer for about 8 years now, starting off with a wacky 2-year-long Mongoose Traveller 1E game that formed my basis of the hobby. I've since dabbled in a few systems - with a particular soft spot for one-pagers like Honey Heist/A Familiar Problem - and have landed at the [BREAK!!](https://breakrpg.com/) system as my comfort choice. 

BREAK!!'s versatility in throwing anything at it - theme, tones, mechanics - and it landing perfectly, alongside its community, have made me fall in love with it. I've been running a 2-year-long campaign in it, and have been playtesting many of the one-shots from my [collection](http://its.quagg.studio/break.html#helical-expeditions) the past few months.

Besides that, I love building niche automated tools for the systems I play (perhaps evidenced by the pages on this website) and building integrations between open-source tools out there.

### My BREAK!! Campaign:
Without blabbing too much, my longer BREAK!! campaign is set on a relatively small island called Skyray Isle within the Galvanus Archipelago - with a few larger settlements that exist but are mostly isolated from one another. It contains a mishmash of all biomes (desert to the north, jungle to the south, etc.) by means of a massive sheer cliff that sections the island in half. Its older inhabitants claim that the island itself used to be a massive flying, living machine of the 3rd Aeon Gleysian Empire and thus the cliff is its spine. No one can quite confirm that, though :)

It is currently in a state of chaos as the once-banished Idea of Thorns (from the amazing pointcrawl [Garden of Ynn](https://soulmuppetpublishing.itch.io/the-gardens-of-ynn)!) has made its way back into the world by hitchhiking on the party's back. The party is, ah, trying their best to sort that all out.
